Curriculum

About the NEB Curriculum

🎓 NEB Affiliated

All programmes follow the National Examination Board (NEB) curriculum. Students appear in Grade 11 and Grade 12 board examinations conducted by NEB, Government of Nepal.

📚 Two-Year Programme

The +2 level spans two years — Class 11 and Class 12 — after completing SEE (Secondary Education Examination, formerly SLC). Stream selection is made at admission.

🔬 Lab & Practical Work

Science students benefit from well-equipped Physics, Chemistry, and Biology laboratories. Regular practical sessions complement theory, as required by the NEB examination pattern.

🏆 Board Exam & Results

Our students consistently achieve excellent results in NEB board examinations. Dedicated exam preparation, mock tests, and revision classes are conducted before board exams.
