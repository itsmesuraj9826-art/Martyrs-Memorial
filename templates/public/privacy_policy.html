{{ school_name }} ("we", "our", or "the school"), located at Biratnagar-10, College Road, Nepal, is committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of students, parents, staff, and website visitors. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how it is handled.

By using our website or enrolling at our institution, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1 Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal identification — name, date of birth, gender, nationality, and photograph of students and guardians.
  • Contact details — address, phone number, and email address of students, parents, and staff.
  • Academic records — examination results, attendance records, progress reports, and disciplinary records.
  • Financial information — fee payment records (no credit/debit card data is stored on our servers).
  • Website usage data — IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time spent, collected automatically via server logs.
  • Contact form submissions — name, email, phone, and message when you contact us through our website.

2 How We Use Information

Information collected is used strictly for educational and administrative purposes, including:

  • Processing admissions, enrolment, and examination registrations.
  • Communicating important academic notices, results, and fee reminders.
  • Maintaining accurate student records as required by the National Examination Board (NEB) of Nepal.
  • Responding to enquiries submitted through our contact form.
  • Improving the quality and accessibility of our website.
  • Complying with applicable laws and regulations of Nepal.
We do not use personal information for marketing, advertising, or commercial profiling purposes.

3 Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent personal information to third parties. Information may be shared only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Regulatory bodies — with NEB and the Government of Nepal as required for examination and affiliation purposes.
  • Legal obligation — when required by law, court order, or government authority.
  • Service providers — with trusted third-party vendors (e.g., SMS notification services) who are bound by confidentiality agreements.
  • Emergency situations — to protect the safety and welfare of a student or others.

4 Cookies

Our website uses minimal cookies to enhance your browsing experience:

  • Session cookies — temporary cookies that expire when you close your browser, used to maintain login sessions for admin users.
  • Preference cookies — stored in your browser's localStorage to remember your dark/light mode preference.

We do not use third-party tracking cookies or advertising cookies. You may disable cookies in your browser settings; however, some features of the site (such as admin login) may not function correctly without them.

5 Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse, including:

  • Password-protected admin access with secure hashing.
  • Regular backups of critical school records.
  • Restricted physical and digital access to student files.

While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information, no method of internet transmission is 100% secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6 Children's Privacy

As an educational institution, we necessarily collect information about minors (students under 18). All such information is collected with the explicit consent of a parent or legal guardian at the time of admission. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors through our website without parental consent.

Student photographs or achievement details published on the website (e.g., toppers section) are done so only with written consent from the student's guardian.

7 Your Rights

Students, parents, and staff have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of personal information held by the school.
  • Correction — request correction of inaccurate or incomplete records.
  • Deletion — request removal of personal data where there is no legitimate reason for its continued retention (subject to legal requirements).
  • Objection — object to the processing of personal data in certain circumstances.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@martyrsmemorial.edu.np or visit the school office during working hours (Sun–Fri, 6 AM – 5 PM).

8 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable laws. When we do, the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will be revised. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the school's services after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
